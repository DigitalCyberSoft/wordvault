🔐 Word Vault

A vocabulary game disguised as safe-cracking. Five confusable words, one sentence with a hole in it — pick the word that truly fits, five in a row, and the vault swings open.

All five words fit the sentence's grammar — only one fits its meaning. The clue is in the reading: worn out, slowly, the whole team together. Every correct word seats a brass pin; one wrong word and all the pins spring back out. A safe grants three runs, then it keeps its secret, shows you the words that got away, and the game gently steps down a level.

Get one wrong and it teaches instead of scolding: the word that fits and the word you picked appear side by side with their dictionary meanings, the cue, and one line on the difference. Cracked safes come back later as little review lockboxes with fresh sentences, so the words stick.

It adapts by itself

Clean cracks raise the level, stuck safes lower it, and the game settles where the reader is challenged but never stuck. Early levels use words every child knows in clearly different jobs (hiss / moo / bark); the top levels separate words one shade apart (gleaming / twinkling) and the mix-ups school actually marks down: their / there / they're, borrow / lend. There are no timers anywhere — a slow correct answer is a correct answer. Every crack pays a collectible into the Curio Cabinet, and finished shelves unlock new looks for the vault.

✅ Safe & private. No accounts, no ads, no chat, no purchases. The game makes no internet connection at all while playing: progress is saved only in this browser, on this device, and nothing is ever sent anywhere. One switch resets it for a new player.
