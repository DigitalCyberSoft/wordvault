How to play

Five words are carved on the brass tiles. The paper slip shows a sentence with a hole in it. Tap the one word that truly fits. Every correct word seats one tumbler pin — seat them all in a row and the vault swings open. Bigger vaults need more pins as you level up. One wrong word and all the pins spring back: that run is over, a lamp lights on the alarm, and three wrong answers ring the alarm — that safe stays shut for now. Press and hold a tile to peek at what a word means (on lower levels).
